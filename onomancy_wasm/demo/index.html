Onomancy: DNSSEC-rooted names, verified in your tab

The verifier below runs entirely in WebAssembly: it fetches the DNSSEC chain over DNS-over-HTTPS, walks it from the baked-in IANA root keys, and grades the binding — no certificate authority, no trusted resolver, no server of ours. 

…
The transport (Cloudflare DoH by default) is untrusted by design: a lying resolver can cause staleness or rejection, never a false bind. Verification happens against the root keys compiled into the Wasm.
